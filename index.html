The whole course in one breath

Vision opens the course: from Marr's stages to CLIP, which links pictures and words (00). Language is a structured, predictable code for thoughts (01–03). Because it's predictable, we can measure it: Zipf's law describes its shape, and Shannon's entropy measures its surprise (04–06). Turn counts into probabilities and you get language models scored by perplexity (07). Add hidden tags and you get HMM taggers and syntax (08). Then neural networks learn word vectors (09–12), RNNs read sequences (13–14), attention lets a model look back at the important words (15), and the same tools power coreference resolution, question answering and sentiment analysis (16–18).
