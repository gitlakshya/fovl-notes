Self-test

1. What is the entropy of a fair 4-sided die?

log₂4 = 2 bits.

2. Zipf: the 10th most frequent word occurs 500 times. Roughly how often does the 50th occur?

f₁₀ × 10 = f₅₀ × 50, so f₅₀ = 500 × 10/50 = 100.

3. If X and Y are independent, what are I(X;Y) and H(Y|X)?

I = 0, and H(Y|X) = H(Y).

4. Corpus: "<s> a b </s>", "<s> a c </s>". Bigram P(b | a)? Add-one P(c | b) with V = 4 (a, b, c, </s>)?

P(b|a) = C(a b)/C(a) = 1/2. Add-one P(c|b) = (0+1)/(1+4) = 1/5.

5. A sentence of 4 tokens has probability 1/16. Perplexity?

(1/16)−1/4 = 161/4 = 2.

6. Why can back-off be harmful?

An unseen trigram may be unseen because it's ungrammatical or nonsense ("I eats"). Backing off gives it a respectable probability anyway.

7. Skip-gram with negative sampling, window 3 (each side), K = 5. Sigmoids per target occurrence?

6 context words × (1 + 5) = 36.

8. Gold y = [0, 0, 1], prediction p = [0.1, 0.4, 0.5]. Cross-entropy (natural log)?

−ln 0.5 ≈ 0.693.

9. RNN: one-hot input of size 1,000, hidden 128, output over 1,000 words. Count W_xh, W_hh, W_hy.

128,000 + 16,384 + 128,000 = 272,384 (plus biases).

10. NER with 4 entity types using BIO. Number of output labels?

2 × 4 + 1 = 9.

11. Attention: s = (1, 1), h₁ = (1, 0), h₂ = (0, 2). Weights and context vector?

Scores 1, 2 → softmax ≈ [0.269, 0.731]. c ≈ 0.269·(1,0) + 0.731·(0,2) = (0.27, 1.46).

12. Three QA queries with first correct answers at ranks 2, 1 and "not found". MRR?

(1/2 + 1 + 0)/3 = 0.5.

13. HMM: π(N) = 0.5, π(V) = 0.5; P(run|N) = 0.2, P(run|V) = 0.6. Best tag for the one-word sentence "run"?

N: 0.5 × 0.2 = 0.1; V: 0.5 × 0.6 = 0.3, so V.

14. Give FastText trigrams for "cat" (with boundary markers).

<ca, cat, at>
